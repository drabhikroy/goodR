The star data in bright_stars.csv comes from the HYG Database, version
4.1, by David Nash (astronexus), https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database,
and is used under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International License, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.

It keeps the stars of magnitude 4.5 or brighter, about the faintest seen
from a town. Names are each star's proper name, or else its Greek letter
or number and constellation. Distance is converted from parsecs to light
years. Temperature is worked out from the B-V color index (Ballesteros
2012), and color is the color a star of that temperature appears. This
derived file is shared under the same license.
